It was a beautiful Friday afternoon of April 2023, Gorky the founder of Gorky Enterprises™ was at his house Gorky was sitting at his $2999 iDesk made of 102% plastic(table legs and stability are a plus $2890 accessory that Gorky does not have) Gorky Enterprises™ is a 336 story building more than double the height of the Burj Khalifa and is located in Butte, Idaho. All of a sudden Gorky gets an alarming call from the vice president of Gorky Enterprises™ , it was Gorkina who was managing the company over Gorky’s spring break.

Gorkina shouts at Gorky over the phone saying “WE HAVE TO FIX THE STOCK MARKET BEFORE IT CRASHES!”. Gorky slams his $2999 iDesk in panic and the entire thing collapses and shatters into exactly 32 pieces, and of course it is not covered by the $600 apple care plus warranty. Gorky starts screaming at the desk and then decides to go and try to fix the stock market! after he shouts “I’m going to try and fix the stock market!”.

Gorkina who was still on the phone gets worried saying that Gorky’s high blood pressure could cause him to pass out at any time. Gorky says “I’m fine and I will fix this horrible catastrophe”. Before Gorky hangs up the telephone he tells Gorkina to set up an appointment at the Genius bar so that he can go to the genius bar when he returns. He tells Gorkina” schedule it this evening” he then gets in his iCar 32 and drives to Gorky Enterprises™.

Gorky arrives at Gorky Enterprises™ and he hops out of his car before running into Gorky Enterprises™. Everybody is yelling “the stock market is down, call NOLE MUSKET”. Some guy says “isn't that the guy who caused Blue Bird to shut down”. Gorky looks out the office window of Gorky Enterprises™ to see people running around and screaming “oh no we are depresso™!”.

Gorky all of a sudden gets the genius idea of going to the basement of Gorky Enterprises™ to fire up the one and only top secret absolutely diabolical Gorky Enterprises™ Time Machine or the GETM(get-em). Gorky decides to time travel 37 minutes into the past to fix the stock market. Gorky steps into the GETM and Gorkina wishes him best travels and reminds him to take his blood pressure medicaid. Gorky was apprehensive upon using the time machine. He hits the big red button on the time machine without realizing he set the time machine time dial to 37 years and not 37 minutes.

Gorky steps out the time machine and he appears to be still at Gorky enterprises, but the paint in the basement is clear and reads Harris Enterprises™. Gorky tries to use the elevator, but the elevator is just being installed into the building. This forces Gorky to walk up all six-hundred seventy-two flights of stairs. He finally reaches the top of Gorky Enterprises™. Gorky walks through the large double doors and he sees what seems to be the CEO of Harris Enterprises jamming out to Funky Town on his Brand-Spankin’-New™ Sony Walkman WM-D3. Whoever this former CEO was, he was such a funky-monkey.

Gorky approaches the funky CEO and he asks the CEO who he is and the CEO replies “I am the great one and only Harris, the founder and CEO of this fine company.” Wow said Gorky I thought that you were an urban legend of Gorky Enterprises™. It is an honor to meet you, Gorkina would be so proud of me. Harris then asked who Gorky was and why he was supposedly talking about Gorky Enterprises™.

Gorky then said that he should explain his story with a big pot of coffee. Harris grabs a fresh pot of coffee and two very twisty straws, and they make lots of intense and loud slurping noises as they talk. Gorky was talking for so long that he had forgotten why he was there. Gorky asks where he can consume a delicious Whopper from Burger King. Harris states “There is a burger king a mile away from the building and all you have to do to get there is follow your heart.” Gorky walks back down the six-hundred seventy-two flights of stairs to try to find the Gorky mobile. This is when his two brain cells rub together and realizes that his car is replaced by the Harris Mobile (1986 pink spiderman tricycle).

Gorky hops on the one and only somehow possible 1986 pink spiderman tricycle and embarks on his insanely absolutely incredible one mile adventure to the one and only Burger King in Idaho that contains the juiciest most pickle-filled munchin’ crunchin’ Burger King Whopper. After his extremely exhausting and treacherous adventure he finally makes it to the one and only Burger King in Idaho that contains the juiciest most pickle-filled munchin’ crunchin’ Burger King Whopper.

Gorky kicks a pebble on the way in which totally won’t cause a massive butterfly effect and cause a giant wormhole to open like something that would cause an episode of such to occur in the Simpsons. Gorky walks into the one and only Burger King in Idaho that contains the juiciest, most pickle-filled munchin’ crunchin’ Burger King Whopper.

Gorky goes up to the tall male cashier wearing a funky hat on his head. Gorky says “I would like one juicy pickle-filled Whopper pretty please.” The Cashier replies with a loud booming “ one juicy pickle-filled Whopper coming right up!”. Gorky spots a short man who looks like some goblin man dancing all over the burger king lettuce. Gorky finds it absolutely delicious at he’d rate this fine Burger establishment with the highest sanitary rating he could( IF HE WAS A FOOD INSPECTOR).

Gorky is presented with a Burger King bag in his name. This bag must contain the juiciest, most pickle-filled munchin’ crunchin’ Burger King Whopper. Gorky goes to one of the seats in the Burger King. He sits down and opens the Burger King Bag. Then, he pulls out the Burger King Burger Box and he opens up the box. Inside of the box is the juiciest, most pickle-filled munchin’ crunchin’ Burger King Whopper.

This juicy pickle-filled mustard and ketchup splatting, tomato stacking, smelly, juicy and tender patty, on a whole wheat bun with a sesame seed top bun Whopper. Gorky picks it up and takes a munchin’ crunchin’ chomp out of the juicy pickle-filled mustard and ketchup splatting, tomato stacking, smelly, juicy and tender patty, on a whole wheat bun with a sesame seed top bun Whopper. This diabolical chomp out of the burger is the most beautiful, most delicious, most sumptuous bite of a burger gorky has ever consumed.

Gorky is extremely overwhelmed and he puts the burger back inside the Burger King Box. Then, he puts the Burger King Burger Box into the Burger King Burger Bag. He picks up the Burger King Burger Bag containing the Burger King Burger Box with the juiciest, most pickle-filled munchin’ crunchin’ Burger. He walks out the door and unknowingly steps into a massive wormhole outside of the door. There is no telling about how Gorky did not notice this.

He travels through the wormhole and is thrown onto the ground once he exits. He realizes that he is once again in the present once he sees the present day Gorky Enterprises™ building looming over the present day burger king. Since the 1986 pink spiderman tricycle is no longer existent, Gorky has to walk the one mile journey back to the Gorky Enterprises™ building. Gorky walks into the Gorky Enterprises™ building and luckily he doesn't have to walk up the six-hundred seventy-two flights of stairs. He is able to take the amazing beautiful elevator to his office and he has never been so happy. But unfortunately upon calling the elevator he realizes that it must travel down all three-hundred thirty-six floors. Gorky starts jamming to funky town which was still stuck in his head after coming across Harris.

Once the elevator arrives after what felt like forty-seven centuries he is finally ascending the three-hundred thirty-six floors. Gorky steps into his office and he sees Gorkina smiling. She tells Gorky “I have no idea why you are here on your break but if you were somehow curious the stock market is doing better than ever”. Gorky smiles and is jubilant and jumping with joy. He stands peacefully knowing that the stock market is saved.
